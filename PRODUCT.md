# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Young people aged 18–28, Romanian-speaking, who feel lost after school or mid-path: they don't know what comes next, want change but can't start, and compare themselves to peers who seem to have a plan. Most have never tried coaching and don't know what it is. They arrive on phone and desktop in roughly equal measure (no traffic data yet).

## Product Purpose

Personal website of Ștefan Neculicioiu, a youth coach. Its job is to make a first-time visitor feel understood, trust Ștefan enough, and book the free 90-minute discovery session ("ședință gratuită de cunoaștere") on Calendly. Success = discovery sessions booked.

## Positioning

Ștefan is a new coach who is close in age to his clients and went through the same uncertainty himself (finished high school at 19 with no idea what next, six months at an art school in the Netherlands, then psychology). He coaches as a peer: dialogue between equals, without judgment, helping the client find their own answer rather than giving advice. Coaching is explicitly positioned as not therapy, not consulting, not mentoring.

## Operating Context

All sessions are online (Google Meet). Booking is via Calendly (`https://calendly.com/stefanneculicioiu`); secondary contact via email (`stefanneculicioiu@gmail.com`) and WhatsApp (`+40 744 494 721`). Pricing: single session 150 lei (~30 EUR, 60 min); 5-session package 600 lei (~120 EUR) including the free discovery session plus 4 working sessions. Blog exists but all articles are "coming soon" (planned twice a month).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site: plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step; 4 pages (Acasă, Servicii, Blog, Contact).
- All copy is Romanian and is fixed: redesigns change design only, never the wording.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Ștefan Neculicioiu · Youth Coach. Hero line in English: "YOUR JOURNEY, YOUR CLARITY, YOUR ACTION."
- Voice: warm, honest, direct, peer-to-peer; informal "tu".
- Must feel premium and warm, and appropriate for a new coach speaking to people new to coaching.

## Evidence on Hand

## Product Principles

1. Understood before sold: the visitor should recognise themselves before being asked to book.
2. Honest newcomer: credibility comes from Ștefan's own story and training, never from inflated claims.
3. One easy next step: the free discovery session is always the primary, low-pressure action.
4. First-timer friendly: explain coaching simply; nothing intimidating or clinical.
